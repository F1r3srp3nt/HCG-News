# Editorial review before App Store screenshots — 27 November 2026

**Scope:** all 13 articles published on news.hakmicapitalgroup.com on 27 November 2026. Twelve are marked Featured and eight Breaking.

**Method:**
- The text was read from the live site, read-only.
- Factual claims were checked with web searches by three independent reviewers. Some checks rest on search snippets, because several hosts blocked full page fetches; this is marked where it matters.
- Nothing was changed on the publication, and nobody was contacted.

**This is an editorial-risk review, not legal advice.** Have the HIGH items looked at by someone qualified in Norwegian media law before the app launches.

## Why this matters for the app

The app shows the live publication. Whatever is Featured or Breaking on the website appears at the top of the app. At the time of the review, the top story and the breaking ticker included the two highest-risk articles. App Review, and anyone who installs the app, will see them.
