### HCG News app (iPhone and iPad)

**Reading.** You can read HCG News in the app without an account. When you read, the app requests published articles from HCG's servers; like any web request, this involves your device's IP address. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not share data with third parties for advertising.

**On your device.** The following stay on your device and are not sent to us:
- saved articles;
- downloaded articles and their images;
- reading positions;
- your reading, edition and appearance preferences.

Deleting the app removes them.

**Signing in (optional).** If you sign in with your HCG account, the app sends your email address and password to HCG's sign-in service, the same one used by hakmicapitalgroup.com, to create a session.
- The password is not stored.
- The session tokens are stored only in your device's Keychain.
- We use your account identifier and email address to show your account and to check your access, for example whether you have an HCG News newsroom role.
- Signing out ends the session on this device.

Your HCG account itself is managed on hakmicapitalgroup.com.

**Newsroom (HCG News staff only).** If you have a newsroom role, the articles, images and videos you create or upload in the app are stored on HCG's servers as part of the HCG News publishing system, like work done on the website. Unsaved edits are kept on your device until you save or discard them.

**HCG workspaces.** If you open the HCG ERP or another HCG workspace from the app, the app asks HCG for a one-time sign-in link and opens it in Safari. The website's own privacy terms apply there.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time; see the contact page.
